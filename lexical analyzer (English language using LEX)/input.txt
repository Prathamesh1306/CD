The world of Attack on Titan is cruel but beautiful.

Eren Yeager lived peacefully in Shiganshina district until the Colossal Titan appeared.
The massive creature destroyed the outer wall, allowing smaller Titans to enter.

Eren witnessed his mother being devoured helplessly by a smiling Titan.
He swore revenge against all Titans, joining the military with his childhood friends.

The Survey Corps bravely ventures outside the walls, fighting tirelessly against the Titans.
Captain Levi, humanity's strongest soldier, skillfully slaughters Titans effortlessly.

Eren surprisingly discovered his ability to transform into a Titan.
The government initially feared his power, considering him dangerous.

Mikasa Ackerman protects Eren devotedly, demonstrating superhuman strength in battle.
Armin Arlert strategically plans missions, compensating for physical weakness with intelligence.

Commander Erwin Smith sacrificed countless soldiers, believing the truth about their world justified the bloodshed.

Beyond the ocean lies Marley, the kingdom that created the Titan threat originally.
The Eldians suffer discrimination, treated cruelly as descendants of "devils."

The history of Titans connects deeply to ancient conflicts between nations.
Ymir Fritz gained the power of Titans mysteriously, becoming the Founding Titan.

The story explores freedom, sacrifice, nationalism, and the endless cycle of hatred.
War continues relentlessly, claiming lives mercilessly on both sides.

Eren's journey transforms dramatically from victim to warrior to something entirely unexpected.
